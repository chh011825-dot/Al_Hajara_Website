Who we are

A builder's company, run like an estate office.

AL-HAJARA Estate and Builders (Pvt.) Ltd is headquartered in Bahria Town, Karachi, with project teams covering Islamabad and Lahore — close enough to every active site to walk the plot before signing off on it. That same site access lets us represent overseas Pakistanis and international investors who can't be on the ground themselves.

We work both ends of a property's life: acquiring and verifying land, and building on it to a standard we'd accept for our own family. Under C.E.O. Sadaqat Ali, the company keeps its site count small enough that every project still gets his direct attention.

What we do

Three disciplines, one site team.

From the first survey to the final key handover, AL-HAJARA keeps estate dealing and construction under one roof — so nothing gets lost in a handoff between departments.

01

Estate & Plot Dealing

Buying, selling and transfer of residential and commercial plots across Islamabad, Lahore and Karachi, with full title verification before any booking.

02

Construction & Development

Grey-structure to finishing for homes, plazas and commercial units — engineered drawings, licensed contractors, and a fixed material specification sheet shared upfront.

How a project runs

Site to signature, in four stages.

The same sequence for a single plot transfer as for a full building contract — only the scale changes.

STAGE 01

Consultation

We walk the plot or the brief with you and map what's realistic against budget and timeline.

STAGE 02

Verification

Title checks, approvals and drawings are finalised and shared before a single rupee changes hands.

STAGE 03

Execution

Construction or transfer proceeds against the published schedule, with weekly site sign-off.

STAGE 04

Handover

Keys, documents and a completion warranty are handed over together — nothing follows later.
